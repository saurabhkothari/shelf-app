SHELF - book manager (no Firebase, no backend)

Files: index.html, manifest.json, sw.js, icon.png
Books are stored in the phone browser. Use Export JSON now and then.

HOST FROM A PHONE (GitHub Pages, free):
1. In your phone browser open github.com, sign in, tap + > New repository, name it 'shelf', set Public, create.
2. Tap 'uploading an existing file', choose the 4 files (index.html, manifest.json, sw.js, icon.png), Commit.
3. Settings > Pages > Branch: main, folder: / (root) > Save.
4. After ~1 minute open https://YOURNAME.github.io/shelf/ on your phone.
5. iPhone: Share > Add to Home Screen.

ISBN lookup (Open Library, then Google Books) needs internet at scan time.
